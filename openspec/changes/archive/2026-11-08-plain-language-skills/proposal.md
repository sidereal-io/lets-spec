## Why

The skills name ISO plain language, but their guidance emphasizes sentence
style and leaves reader tasks implicit. People and fresh agents need to
find, understand and use the same instructions throughout the workflow (D2).

## What Changes

- Apply one plain-language method to all 13 skills and their outputs (D3).
- Revise unclear wording, navigation and presentation while preserving
  workflow rules and exact machine-readable formats (D5).
- Give writing steps, templates and delegated reviewers the same criteria.
- Check representative reader tasks and report the limits of those checks
  instead of treating sentence length as evidence of clarity (D6).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `plain-language`: Guidance and review criteria for communications that
  people and agents can find, understand and use.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing workflow, domain-language and review boundaries still apply.

## Impact

Source skills, their references and templates, schema instructions, and the
shared writing rule in `overlays/AGENTS.md`. Both release flows must carry
working reference links. Existing interface and release checks protect
workflow contracts. This story belongs to epic #194 and closes #224.
